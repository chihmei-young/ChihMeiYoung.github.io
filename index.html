01 / About me

Curiosity across
scales & disciplines.

I study the tiny building blocks that shape the materials around us.

My doctoral research at the University of Michigan brings together chemical engineering and scientific computing. Working with Professors Michael J. Solomon and Sharon C. Glotzer, I investigate the functional properties of self-assembled colloids using experiments and molecular dynamics simulations.

Previously, I earned my bachelor’s and master’s degrees in Chemical Engineering at National Tsing Hua University in Taiwan, where I explored dendrimer–surfactant complexes with Professor Hsin-Lung Chen.

Beyond the lab, I enjoy making science accessible — from mentoring undergraduate students to helping high school students build colorful colloidal crystal keychains.
